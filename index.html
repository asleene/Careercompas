Entrance exams to research

Examples include JEE Main for many engineering routes, NEET-UG for MBBS/BDS and other notified courses, CUET-UG for participating universities, NIFT/NID admissions for selected design programmes, and state-level admissions. Requirements vary by course and year.

Do not assume every exam is needed. Start with the exact course you want and read its official eligibility bulletin.

🏫

How to compare affordable colleges

  • Compare total cost: tuition, hostel, transport, books and exam fees.
  • Check official recognition/approval and the course syllabus.
  • Ask about scholarships, fee waivers, instalments and placement disclosures.
  • Consider a nearby public institution if it offers the right course.

MAKE IT PRACTICAL

A simple study and preparation roadmap

Adjust this template to your class, school calendar, and chosen course.

1

Weeks 1–2: Explore

Choose 2–3 careers, read what people actually do, note subjects and prerequisites, and speak to a teacher or professional if possible.

2

Weeks 3–4: Compare

Compare streams, course lengths, costs, location, entrance requirements, scholarships, and backup options.

3

Month 2: Try a small project

Try a beginner lesson, science activity, spreadsheet, writing sample, design project, volunteering activity, or hands-on skill related to your interests.

4

Monthly: Review and ask

Check official updates, track what you enjoy, ask for feedback, and revise the plan as you learn more. Keep school learning and wellbeing balanced.
